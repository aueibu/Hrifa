Z-Pair Shapes

Every pattern we know that two different note sets can share while having exactly the same intervals. A pattern is abstract: pick a ratio for each of its directions and it becomes real JI pairs. The q shown is the smallest intervallic odd limit at which the pattern first occurs in the families found so far.

set P set Q (same intervals, different set) grid dots: lattice points in the pattern's own coordinates

Drawings: 2-D patterns are shown in a compact basis (a change of basis with integer entries and determinant ±1 does not change the pattern). The four 2-D 9-note patterns are drawn as their smallest known example on the 3 (across) by 5 (up) lattice. The 3 × 3 product is drawn as chord A (small triangle) repeated at each note of chord B for P, and of B mirrored for Q. Hover a note for its coordinates.
