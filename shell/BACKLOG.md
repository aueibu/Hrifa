  - **Costs and risks:** eye travel (cursor, then panel); permanent screen space or an open/close control; hold-to-play (hands and eyes are on the pointed thing while a key is held, so looking away to read is awkward: the bubble beside the cursor still wins there); content flicker when sweeping across dense marks (needs a short hold after the pointer leaves, like the lingering roughness readout); screen readers need an `aria-live` region; the Developer menu's delay and on/off settings need rethinking.
  - **Suggestion:** build it as an OPTION (a Developer setting switching between "bubble beside the cursor" and "fixed panel", both reading the same content), not a replacement.
  - **Effect on other work:** the native tooltips arc stopped after its M1 (the seven `title=` attributes). The seven SVG `<title>` tooltips (`DissonanceView`, `RoughnessView`, `RingStats`, `LatticePanel`, `ModeRoot`) are deliberately NOT converted to per-mark bubbles: decide this panel first, because it would make that work unnecessary.
