### Mechanisms (5–6 notes, `research/ji-z-partners/linring_analyze.py`)
Per rhythm pair, best alignment (most shared onsets); the moved part D_A → D_B classified. 5 notes (2,177 rhythm pairs): **shift by half the ring 2,016 (93%)**, by a quarter 93, by a third 21, other 47; 1,986 move one note. 6 notes (67,731): **half-ring shift 52,334 (77%)**, third 2,956, quarter 2,861, larger orders ~150, mirror 4, other 9,426 (14%); 58,714 move two notes. Why the half-ring shift works: A = X + D, B = X + (D + L/2); distances inside X and inside D are unchanged, and each cross distance d becomes L/2 − d, so A ~ B exactly when the cross-distance multiset X–D is symmetric about L/4 (group ring: XD* + DX* fixed by the order-2 element). On the ratios, a half-ring shift adds 1/2 (5/4 → 7/4), a third adds 1/3, a quarter 1/4: an arithmetic move, unrelated to the JI factor law. Ring 81 (odd) has no half-ring shift and no 5-note pairs (canon: 5 onsets need a ring divisible by 2 or 5); ring 80 has no third-ring shifts. The 14% "other" at 6 notes is not explained yet.
