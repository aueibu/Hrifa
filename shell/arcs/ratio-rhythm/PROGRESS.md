## Linear-ring Z-pairs among re-rooted modes (2026-11-21, the human's question "among reroot modes?")
Definitions agreed with the human: a voicing is a ratio set containing 1/1 on its own ring (L = exact LCD, slot = rL − L); a pair has the same L and the same multiset of circular slot distances (this is what makes the canon's 72 pair match; plain distances do not) and is not a ring rotation/reflection; "within" = both voicings are re-rootings of one scale (same odd-harmonic form), "cross" = two scales; **count both, list separately** (human). Each scale pair carries a table of all rooting combinations that match, computed from every mode (not limited to the gamut). Tool: `research/ji-z-partners/linring.py` (`search`, `pair`). PARI/GP not needed for this (direct enumeration).
