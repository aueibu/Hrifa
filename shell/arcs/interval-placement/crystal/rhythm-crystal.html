Rhythm Crystals

Rolf Wallin builds a crystal chord by stacking one germ interval on every note made by another. In time, that is a canon: play cell 1 starting at every onset of cell 2 (and of cell 3), on a cycle of pulses. Where copies land on the same pulse the attack gets heavier, so the result is a rhythm with its own accents. Mirroring a lopsided cell gives a Z-twin: a different rhythm with exactly the same time distances between its attacks, accents included.
