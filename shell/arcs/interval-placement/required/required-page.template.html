Required Interval Collections

Name the intervals a collection must contain, the intervals allowed to hang off its notes, and how many notes it has. The page finds every collection that fits: each required interval is its own pair of notes, and every other note hangs off a note already there by one of the hanging intervals. The result is a pitch collection that does not repeat at the octave; play it together as a chord or one after another as a line.

In Schillinger's terms (Book II, 8 B; Book IX, ch. 8) the required notes are the neutral or rest tones, a hung note is an auxiliary leading into the note it hangs from, and a neutral tone with its auxiliaries is a directional unit. His style questions are the filters below: which neutral tones take auxiliaries, at what interval, built upward or downward.
