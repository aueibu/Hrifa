    Friction & restitution references

    Materials and surfaces above are dry, room-temperature midpoints drawn from these general engineering references — real values vary with finish, moisture, and impact speed. Wood restitution and blanket roughness/tile roughness etc. are our own qualitative model, not literature values.

    • Engineering ToolBox, "Friction and Friction Coefficients for Various Material Combinations," engineeringtoolbox.com/friction-coefficients-d_778.html
    • Engineering ToolBox, "Coefficients of Restitution," engineeringtoolbox.com/restitution-coefficients-d_622.html
    • Avallone & Baumeister (eds.), Marks' Standard Handbook for Mechanical Engineers, 11th ed., Table 3.2.1 (friction coefficients of metals)
    • R. Cross, "The bounce of a ball," American Journal of Physics 67, 222 (1999)
    • USDA Forest Products Laboratory, Wood Handbook: Wood as an Engineering Material, Ch. 4 (Janka hardness) & Ch. 5 (friction properties)
    • Nigg & Herzog, Biomechanics of the Musculo-Skeletal System — natural turf traction/friction testing
    • B.S. Gupta, "Friction in Textile Materials," in Friction in Textile Materials (Woodhead Publishing)
