    Friction & restitution references

    Density, elastic modulus, and hardness for the 18 elements, 6 promoted non-metal materials (cast iron, granite, PMMA, sapphire/alumina, nylon, PTFE), and 18 named wood species (16 species plus White Oak and Northern Red Oak — more surface-only materials like tile, concrete, and fabric can be added later the same way) are drawn from the sources below. Every material is available as both an object and a surface. Restitution is COMPUTED per drop (not looked up) from those properties via a validated elastic-plastic yield model, at the actual impact speed for the selected drop height, for every ductile material; friction uses real measured values where available and a hardness-ranked estimate otherwise. Graphite, granite, PMMA, sapphire/alumina, nylon, PTFE, and all wood species are brittle ceramics/rock, polymers, or anisotropic wood outside this model's validated ductile range — their values are flagged literature-informed placeholders, not computed. Real friction/restitution vary with finish, moisture, and impact speed regardless of source; see the project's ASM_Material_Properties_Reference.xlsx reference workbook for the full per-material sourcing and derivation.

    • ASM Handbook Vol. 1, Properties and Selection: Irons, Steels, and High-Performance Alloys
    • ASM Handbook Vol. 2, Properties and Selection: Nonferrous Alloys and Special-Purpose Materials — "Properties of Pure Metals" and "Refractory Metals and Alloys" articles
    • ASM Handbook Vol. 8, Mechanical Testing and Evaluation
    • CRC Handbook of Chemistry and Physics, Internet Edition — measured dry sliding-friction table
    • W.J. Stronge, Impact Mechanics, 2nd ed. (Cambridge University Press, 2018)
    • K.L. Johnson, Contact Mechanics (Cambridge University Press, 1985)
    • J. Coaplen, W.J. Stronge & B. Ravani, "Work equivalent composite coefficient of restitution," International Journal of Impact Engineering 30(6), 581–592 (2004)
    • Engineered Materials Handbook, Desk Edition (compiled Vols. 1–4) — cast iron, granite, PMMA, sapphire/alumina, nylon, and PTFE properties
    • F. Cardarelli, Materials Handbook, 3rd ed. (Springer, 2018) — cast iron, granite, PMMA, sapphire/alumina, nylon, and PTFE properties
    • Tokai Carbon and Olmec isostatic-graphite datasheets (web search, carbon/graphite density & modulus only — not in any of the above)
    • USDA Forest Products Laboratory, Wood Handbook: Wood as an Engineering Material — density, elastic modulus, Poisson's ratio (oak species), and CRC-referenced friction for the 18 named wood species
    • George Hart, Virtual Polyhedra data, via the finnp/polyhedra dataset (github.com/finnp/polyhedra) — vertex coordinates for the 8 prism, 8 antiprism, 13 Archimedean, and 92 Johnson solid shapes; the 13 Catalan solid shapes are computed by this app as the polar dual of each Archimedean solid, not separately sourced
